Answers

Straight answers.

So what does XYLO do?

It is designed to let holders of on-chain equities lock them into a pool, receive an advance as each cycle opens, and stay exposed to price swings of what they locked.

Where does the advance come from?

From counterparties who make offers for the upside you agree to give up. If offers come in too low, the pool can skip a cycle.

Price rallies, then what?

You capture gains as far as the line you chose. Anything beyond that line is claimed by the winning counterparty.

And if it drops?

The advance softens the blow slightly, but you continue to carry the decline of the underlying shares, as any owner would.

Is leaving early possible?

The plan is to let you exit at a cycle boundary, with a request window ahead of cut-off. Details will be confirmed ahead of launch.

Is there a cost?

No pricing is set yet. Any cost will be published ahead of taking effect.
