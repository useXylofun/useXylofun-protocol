# XYLO Whitepaper

Early draft. Figures marked TBD are placeholders until real numbers exist.

## Vision and mission

Holders of on-chain equities usually leave them idle. XYLO's vision is a world where those holdings can earn on a schedule without being sold. Its mission is to build a Solana pool where holders lock equities, collect an advance as each seven-day cycle opens, and keep their price exposure.

## How it is meant to work

1. Lock an on-chain equity into a pool.
2. Choose a reach: how much upside to trade away for the advance.
3. Collect the advance as the cycle opens; the cycle close settles the rest.

- **Phase 3, Launch:** audited contracts, mainnet pools, additional lanes (patient bid, two-step).
- **Phase 4, Expansion:** open desk bidding, basket lane, floor-protected lane.
